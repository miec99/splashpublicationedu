Services

What we help with

Every student is different, so every plan starts with a conversation. These are the areas we work on with families.

Career counselling

Psychometric assessment and one-on-one discussion to choose a field of study and a career direction that fits the student.

University selection

A balanced shortlist of universities and courses, matched to the student's profile, goals, budget and preferred countries.

Undergraduate admissions

Applications to universities in the US, UK, Canada, Europe, Asia and Australia, including Common App, UCAS and direct applications.

Masters, MBA and PhD

Admissions strategy, statements of purpose, resumes and interview preparation for graduate programmes and working professionals.

Essays and documents

Guidance and editing for essays, personal statements, SOPs, resumes and recommendation briefs. The writing stays the student's own.

Profile building and mentorship

Long-term mentoring from Grade 8 to 12: subject choices, activities, projects, competitions and summer programmes.

Test planning

Advice on which tests to take and when: SAT, ACT, IELTS, TOEFL, GRE and GMAT, planned around application deadlines.

Scholarships and pre-departure

Help with scholarship applications, visa document checklists and preparing to leave home. We are not a visa or immigration agent.

Frequently asked questions

Can sessions be held online?

Yes. Consultations and follow-up sessions are held by video call, so students anywhere in India or abroad can work with us. Sessions in person in Mumbai are available by appointment.

When should a student start?

Any stage works, but earlier is better. Students who start in Grade 8 or 9 have time to explore careers and build a strong profile. Masters and MBA applicants usually start 9 to 12 months before their intended intake.

Do you guarantee admission?

No. Admission, scholarship and visa decisions are made by universities and governments, and no consultant can promise them. Our job is to help each student put forward the strongest honest application they can.

Which countries do you cover?

The United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Europe, Singapore, Hong Kong, Australia and India.

How do I pay, and do I get an invoice?

You can pay online by card, UPI or net banking, or by bank transfer. GST is charged at 18% and a GST invoice is issued for every payment.
